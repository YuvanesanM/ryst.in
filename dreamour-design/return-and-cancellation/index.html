Return & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This policy explains refunds and cancellations for design services and products from Dreamour Multiventures. It applies in addition to any written agreement you have with us. If your agreement says something different, the agreement applies. Nothing here affects your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

1. Design services

1.1 Consultation and design-phase fees

  • Fees paid for an initial consultation are non-refundable once the consultation has taken place.
  • If you cancel during the design phase, we refund any amount paid in excess of the value of work completed up to the date of cancellation, as set out in your agreement.

1.2 Drawings and deliverables

  • Architectural plans, drawings, 3D visualisations and other design deliverables are prepared specifically for you, so they cannot be returned or refunded once delivered.
  • Revisions are included as described in your agreement. Please review each stage carefully and share feedback, so the final deliverables meet your expectations.

1.3 Cancelling services

  • You may cancel by writing to info@dreamour.in. Fees for work completed until the cancellation date, and any third-party costs already incurred for you, remain payable.
  • If we have to cancel an engagement, we refund fees paid for work not yet carried out.

2. Products

2.1 Products bought on Amazon

Returns, replacements and refunds for our cookware and appliances bought on Amazon India are handled through Amazon, under Amazon's return policy. Please start the request from your Amazon order.

2.2 Products bought directly from us

  • Standard products: may be returned within 7 days of delivery for a full refund, excluding shipping charges. They must be unused, in original condition, with all packaging and tags.
  • Custom or made-to-order products (including bespoke furniture): can't be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective, or materially different from the agreed specification. Report this within 7 days of delivery, with photos, and we'll arrange a repair, replacement or refund.
  • Cancelling an order: possible within 6 hours of placing it, or at any time before dispatch for standard products. Custom work that has already started may carry costs for materials and work completed.

3. Refunds

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method within 7 working days of approval, or of our receiving the returned product. Your bank may take a few more days to credit the amount.

4. How to request a return or cancellation

  1. Email info@dreamour.in or WhatsApp +91 96001 25599 with your name, order or project reference, and the reason.
  2. We acknowledge your request within 48 hours and tell you the next steps.
  3. If you're not satisfied with how your request is handled, write to our Grievance Officer, named in the Privacy Policy. We aim to resolve complaints within one month.

5. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The version in force when you placed your order or signed your agreement applies to it.
