2. Personal data we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiries and project briefs. The forms on this Website do not save anything. When you press send, your WhatsApp or email app opens with your answers filled in, and we receive them only if you send that message. This may include your name, phone number, email address, the type of project and scope, the project location and size, an approximate budget and timeline, and anything else you write.
  • Calls, WhatsApp and email. Your contact details and whatever you share with us in the conversation.
  • Clients. When you engage us: your name and contact details, project and site address, site photographs and measurements, drawings and documents you provide, billing details such as GSTIN, and payment records.

Information collected automatically

  • Hosting logs. The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, it receives technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and may log it for security and to operate the service. We do not use this data to identify you. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • No analytics or advertising. We do not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, fingerprinting or similar tracking.

Some of our service providers (such as GitHub, Meta and Google) may process data on servers outside India. Such transfers are made as permitted under the DPDP Act and are subject to any restrictions notified by the Government of India.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to a project: up to 24 months from our last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Client and project records: for the duration of the project and for as long as needed afterwards to support the completed work and meet legal obligations.
  • Invoices and accounting records: for the period required by tax and accounting laws, including the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017.

When personal data is no longer needed for its purpose, or you withdraw consent and no legal reason to keep it applies, we erase it.

7. How we protect it

We take reasonable security practices and procedures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, use, disclosure, alteration or loss. These include HTTPS encryption on the Website, access-controlled accounts with strong authentication, and limiting access to people who need it. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify affected individuals and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we process it, and the identities of those we have shared it with.
  • Correct, complete or update inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erase data that is no longer needed, unless we must keep it by law.
  • Withdraw consent at any time. This is as easy as giving it, and does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew.
  • Grievance redressal through our Grievance Officer (see below).
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any right, email info@dreamour.in. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

Our services are meant for adults, and this Website is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 without verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.
