Information Collected via Cookies

We use cookies to enhance the browsing and shopping experience on design.dreamour.in. “Cookies” are small files or records that we place on your computer's hard drive to collect information about your activities on design.dreamour.in site. The cookies transmit this information back to the computers at design.dreamour.in or our third-party distributors of banners and newsletters; these computers are, generally speaking, the only computers which are authorized to read such information. The information captured makes it possible for us:

  • to speed navigation, keep track of items in your shopping cart, and provide you with custom tailored content

  • to monitor the effectiveness of certain of our marketing email campaigns
  • to monitor total number of visitors, pages viewed, and the total number of banners served.

Most people do not know that cookies are being placed on their computers when they visit design.dreamour.in site or most web sites because browsers are typically set to accept cookies. You can choose to have your browser warn you every time a cookie is being sent to you or you can turn off cookie placement. If you refuse cookies, you will not be able to open a design.dreamour.in Shopping Cart and therefore will not be able to complete an order with us online. Also, by not using cookies, your overall Internet browsing experience will be affected.
