Questions

Good to know.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. We'll meet you, understand the space and your needs, and talk through ideas and an approximate budget — with no obligation to go ahead.

How much does an interior project cost?

It depends on size, scope and the materials you choose. After the consultation and design stage we share an itemised quote, so you can see exactly what's included and adjust choices to fit your budget.

How long does a project take?

A single room or kitchen typically takes a few weeks; a full home takes longer depending on civil work and scope. We give you a timeline with the quote and keep you updated as we go.

Can you work with my existing furniture?

Absolutely. We often blend pieces you love into the new design — it saves money and keeps the space feeling like yours.

Do you only offer design, or execution too?

Both. Choose full turnkey design-and-build, or a design-only package with drawings and 3D renders you can hand to your own contractor.

Which areas do you serve?

We work across EDIT_CITY and nearby areas. For projects further away, get in touch and we'll let you know what's possible.
